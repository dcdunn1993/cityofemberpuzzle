CHAPTER 7 • GROUP INVESTIGATION • 25–30 MINUTES

The Torn-Message Mystery

Put the scraps of a fictional emergency notice in order, restore its missing words, and investigate what Lina knows about her damaged Instructions in The City of Ember.

Reminder: This message is teacher-created practice material, not the actual Instructions from the novel. It does not reveal later chapters.

Set up your group

One Chromebook can be used per group. Share the screen, discuss your choices, and export one answer file at the end.

Team jobs

Reader • Arranger • Clue Finder • Recorder

You can click scraps and then click numbered slots, or drag and drop with a mouse or trackpad. Your answers save in this browser when possible.

01. Rebuild the notice

Read all eight scraps. Select one and click a numbered slot, or drag it into place. Click an already-placed scrap to move it elsewhere. Use transitions and sentence endings as clues.

Choose one word for each blank. Three words in the bank are decoys and will not be used. Focus on meaning and grammar.
